What is FHIR?

FHIR stands for Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources — a modern way to structure health data for APIs, with standard mappings for legacy HL7v2 feeds.

Just JSON

Resources written in plain JSON, connected as APIs. They drop straight into the stack you already run.

HL7v2 & FHIR

The same clinical concept maps to pipe-delimited HL7v2 or FHIR JSON, depending on what the receiving system speaks. Your HL7v2 knowledge still applies; only the syntax changes.

Reuse & extend

Profiles and extensions let you constrain the standard resources instead of rebuilding them, and reuse the pieces anywhere in your integrations.

Stable & future-proof

Years in development and already running in production at major EHR vendors, anchored by regulation. Built to keep evolving, so what you learn now stays worth knowing.
